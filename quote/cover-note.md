# Cover note

Short text for the remarks field of an online quote form, or the body of an email. The full
request is `README.md` inside the zip.

```
Request for quotation: 6-layer rigid-flex with any-layer HDI, with assembly.

Strip 39.9 x 5.6 mm, eight rigid areas joined by a two-layer flex (layers 3-4), bent once
at installation. Laser microvias 0.10 mm hole / 0.20 mm pad, stacked, filled and capped
via in pad (0.30 mm pitch WLCSP and 0201 pads). Track and gap 0.075 mm. ENIG. No silkscreen.

Please quote 20, 100 and 500 pieces, bare and assembled with X-ray of the WLCSP joints.
The layout is representative, not final: we will adapt it to your stackup and rules.
The attached zip has Gerber, drill, placement and parts files, and a README with the full
specification and eight questions on your process. Please answer those with the quote.
```
