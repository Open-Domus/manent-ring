# Power budget

Generated by `tools/power_budget.py`. Nothing here is measured: the inputs are vendor
figures, datasheet values and assumptions, each marked in the table. The first bench
session on a development kit replaces them.

## Standby alone

| State | Current (estimate) | 15.5 mAh cell lasts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| slow advertising, 1-2 s interval | 15 uA | 34 days |
| connected, idle | 20 uA | 26 days |
| off, button wakes it | 3 uA | 172 days |

Not counted: the quiescent current of the power chip and of the cell protector (a few
microamps together), radio activity while recording, flash reads during upload, and the
cell's own self-discharge, which over months is of the same order as the standby figures.
Upload assumes 7.2 MB per recorded hour sent at 400 kbit/s.

## What to measure first

1. Opus encoder duty cycle on the nRF54L15 at complexity 0: it is the largest term and a guess.
2. PDM and oscillator current while recording.
3. LED current at which the wearer can see it through the resin in daylight.
4. Advertising and connection current with the final intervals.
