# Space budget

Generated by `tools/space_budget.py` from the model in `mechanical/`. Lengths in millimetres.
Part sizes come from `parts/parts.yaml`; several are still unverified there, and the cell
figures are catalogue values, not a supplier's drawing.

## Rules the model applies

- Titanium shell 0.40 thick on the outer face and the side walls; resin skin 0.35 towards the finger.
- At least 0.10 of resin around every part.
- Cell: 8% thickness growth allowed for, plus 0.05 of wrap on each side.
- Flex 5.6 wide; islands joined by 1.0 of bare flex; 3.0 for the cell tabs.
- The button must land on the thumb side (-15 to 30 degrees), microphone and light on the back
  of the finger (60 to 120 degrees).
